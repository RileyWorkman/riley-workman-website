About

I like systems that have to work in the real world — where the math meets friction, backlash, and noisy sensors. My background spans dual-arm manipulation, embedded firmware, computer vision, and simulation, built through capstone robotics, a humanoid-arm research publication, and a wearable ASL-translation glove. Before engineering, I spent years as an actuarial and risk analyst running stochastic and Monte Carlo models — training that now shows up in how I approach uncertainty, reliability, and failure analysis (DFMEA/FMECA) in hardware.
